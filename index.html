A tiny basketball court right on your desktop.

The game opens as a transparent layer over your screen: grab the ball, flick it towards the hoop, score. When you need your desktop back, minimize it to the tray — the ball will wait for you.

Download — it's free Open on itch.io

Windows and Linux · no ads, no internet connection, no data collection

Something to do with your hands

Perfect for phone calls, waiting for a download, or a break between two tasks. Videos keep playing under the game — watch a movie and shoot a few hoops at the same time.

A throw that feels right

The ball flies the way your hand moved. It bounces off the rim, the walls and the floor, squashes on impact, and the net catches it when you score.

Balls with a character

A light and bouncy tennis ball, a floaty volleyball, a bowling ball that is hard to throw at all — and a pumpkin that burns in flight.

Put the hoop anywhere

Drag the hoop and the scoreboard to any spot on the screen. With two monitors, choose the one the game lives on.

17 achievements

Score off a wall, hit a streak, fly a marathon — 42.195 km of total ball flight. Only real throws count, so they are worth earning.

Long shots get noticed

Score from far away and the ring lights up with a burst of sparks. Your longest basket stays on the scoreboard, in metres.

Light and private

Practically no CPU use while the ball is resting. No account, no ads, no network access. 7 languages.
